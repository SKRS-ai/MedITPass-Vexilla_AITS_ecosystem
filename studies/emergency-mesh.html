Emergency Mesh Protocol

Critical Data Retrieval in Zero-Connectivity and Unconscious Patient Scenarios.

The "Golden Hour" Problem

In emergency medicine, the first 60 minutes are critical. Often, first responders arrive at scenes where the patient is non-verbal or unconscious. Knowing a blood type or a severe penicillin allergy can be the difference between life and death. MedPass™ ensures this data is never "locked" behind an unconscious mind.

The Ghost Profile

Read-Only Emergency Burst

Authorized First Responder terminals can trigger a "Ghost Burst" from a locked device. This pulls only the absolute vitals: Blood Type, Allergies, DNR status, and Emergency Contacts. No other personal data is exposed.

Mesh Networking

Zero-Network Connectivity

If cellular towers are down (natural disasters/infrastructure failure), MedPass™ switches to Vexilla Mesh. Devices communicate directly via Bluetooth/NFC to pass the Intake DNA to the paramedic's tablet.

NFC Wearables & Bio-Integration

For high-risk patients (elderly, diabetic, heart patients), MedPass™ extends the Vexilla Shield to wearable hardware. An encrypted NFC wristband or medical tag acts as a passive node, allowing EMTs to scan the patient's wrist and receive the full "Intake DNA" into their mobile response unit instantly.

Legal Protection: The Digital Good Samaritan

The MedPass™ Emergency Protocol is governed by the Good Samaritan Digital Act framework. By providing verified, accurate medical data during a crisis, first responders are shielded from liability, and patients are afforded the highest level of emergency precision ever engineered.
