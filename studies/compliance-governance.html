The Governance Paradox

Traditional medical systems struggle with the "Privacy vs. Accountability" paradox. To be HIPAA compliant, you need an audit trail; however, centralized audit trails are themselves massive privacy risks. MedPass™ resolves this by utilizing Cryptographic Proofs of Exchange. We log that a handshake occurred, without ever seeing the data within the handshake.

Immutable Audit Trails

Every time a patient uses MedPass™ to check in, a Non-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) log is generated. This log proves that the patient authorized the transfer, provides a timestamp, and records the clinic's digital signature. This metadata is essential for clinical accountability but contains zero health data.

The Right to Revoke

Unlike paper records which are permanent once shared, the MedPass™ Governance Framework allows patients to view which clinics have accessed their data and manage future permissions. This is the ultimate expression of data sovereignty: the patient as the "Admin" of their own biological history.

Professional Liability & Malpractice

For medical groups, MedPass™ provides a "Defense Layer." By proving that the data received was the exact data authorized by the patient, clinics are protected from liability claims involving "patient miscommunication" or "clerical errors." The system acts as a neutral, digital witness to the intake process.
