The Handshake Protocol™

A technical breakdown of the secure, zero-latency data burst between the Patient Vault and Clinical EHR systems.

01. The Proximity Trigger

Unlike traditional cloud-based systems that require login credentials and manual searches, the MedPass Handshake is triggered by Physical Proximity. We utilize a dual-channel approach to ensure 100% reliability in hospital environments.

Step 1: Secure NFC Tap

The patient taps their device to the Vexilla-enabled clinic terminal. This creates a peer-to-peer encrypted tunnel (AES-256-GCM).

Step 2: Biometric Gate

The patient's device requests FaceID/Fingerprint authentication. No data leaves the device until the patient physically authorizes the burst.

02. Zero-Knowledge Data Burst

Once authorized, MedPass performs a "Data Burst." Instead of sending the entire medical history, Vexilla AITS identifies the specific Intake Fields requested by the clinic (e.g., Medications, Allergies, Last Procedure) and sends only that packet.

Why it's Secure:

  • No Central Storage: Vexilla servers never hold the unencrypted medical data.
  • Temporary Tunnel: The communication channel collapses instantly once the 3-second burst is complete.
  • Post-Quantum Ready: We utilize Kyber-1024 encryption to ensure that the handshake is immune to future quantum computing threats.

03. Automatic EHR Mapping

On the receiving end, the Vexilla Bridge translates the incoming burst into SMART on FHIR resources. This allows the doctor's existing software (Epic, Cerner, etc.) to read the data as if it were typed in manually, but with 0% transcription error.
