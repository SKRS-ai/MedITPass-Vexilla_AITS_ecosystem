Deployment & Infrastructure

The Operational Blueprint for Transitioning Clinics to Autonomous Intake.

Zero-Friction Implementation

The strength of MedPass™ lies in its Non-Invasive Deployment model. We do not require clinics to rip and replace their existing server architecture. Instead, we introduce a hardware-software "Blanket" that sits atop current workflows, providing an immediate upgrade to data velocity.

Phase 01: Gateway Install

Deployment of the Vexilla Secure Gateway (VSG)—a low-profile hardware node that enables NFC and Encrypted QR handshakes at the front desk and in triage rooms.

Phase 02: EHR Mapping

Our engineering team performs a "Field-to-Field" mapping of the clinic’s specific intake forms into the MedPass Intake DNA™ standard via SMART on FHIR protocols.

Phase 03: Staff Sync

Minimalist training for administrative staff. The workflow shifts from "Data Entry" to "Data Verification," reducing typical onboarding time for new hires by 60%.

Phase 04: Live Go-Live

Concurrent rollout where patients can begin using their Sovereign Medical ID immediately. Paper backups are phased out over a 30-day "Confidence Window."

The Maintenance Cycle: Self-Healing Nodes

Utilizing the Vexilla AITS backbone, every MedPass terminal is a "Self-Healing" node. Security patches and EHR API updates are pushed autonomously from the Vexilla Core, ensuring the clinic is always operating on the latest compliance standards without needing a dedicated on-site IT team.

Autonomous Monitoring Hub

Real-time tracking of Handshake Latency, Encryption Integrity, and EHR Sync Status.

Scalability: From Clinic to Hospital System

Whether it is a single-doctor dental practice or a multi-state hospital network, the deployment protocol remains the same. The Vexilla Technical Blanket scales horizontally, allowing for rapid expansion across entire regions with zero loss in data integrity or security.
