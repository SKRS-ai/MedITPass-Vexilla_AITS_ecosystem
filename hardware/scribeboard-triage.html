The "Clipboard Killer"

The ScribeBoard™ is the physical anchor of the MedPass™ ecosystem. Designed for the high-velocity triage environment, it serves as the secure decryption gateway between the patient's mobile device and the clinic's internal network. No more paper, no more pens, no more contact surfaces.

Equipped with a proprietary Vexilla AITS Chipset, the board performs hardware-level validation of the incoming "Intake DNA" burst before passing it to the EHR system.

Antimicrobial & Industrial

Built for the realities of modern medicine, the ScribeBoard™ features a seamless, chemically-strengthened glass surface that can be sanitized with medical-grade wipes 100+ times daily without degradation. The chassis is impact-resistant (IK10) and features a Kensington lock slot for facility security.

Institutional Scaling

For large hospital systems, ScribeBoards™ can be managed centrally via the Vexilla AITS Fleet Manager, allowing IT departments to update encryption certificates and EHR mapping across thousands of rooms simultaneously.
