ScribeBoard™ Hardware Specs

The Physical Gateway for the Autonomous Handshake Protocol.

The Terminal of the Future

The ScribeBoard™ is a low-latency, industrial-grade medical terminal designed for the high-volume clinical environment. It serves as the physical anchor for the Vexilla AITS Technical Blanket, allowing for millisecond data ingestion from any MedPass™ enabled mobile device or wearable.

Equipped with a high-resolution e-Ink "Privacy Display" and dual-channel NFC sensors, the ScribeBoard™ ensures that the patient's data is only visible to the authorized clinician during the active handshake window.

"Sanitized by Design"

The ScribeBoard™ features a seamless, antimicrobial glass surface rated for medical-grade sterilization. By eliminating physical buttons and ports, we reduce the risk of cross-contamination while maintaining a sleek, modern aesthetic that fits any 2026 practice.

Universal Mounting & Integration

Whether integrated into the Vexilla HomeBase™ for home health monitoring or mounted via VESA-standard brackets in a surgical suite, the ScribeBoard™ communicates back to the clinic's EHR via a secure, whitelisted tunnel. It acts as a "Stateless" input device, meaning no patient data is ever stored on the physical board itself—ensuring that even if the hardware is stolen, the data remains sovereign and secure.
